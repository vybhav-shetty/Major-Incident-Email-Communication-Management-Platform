# Major Incident Email Communication Management Platform

A centralised ServiceNow platform for automating, governing, and streamlining Major Incident email communications. The solution standardises stakeholder communications, reduces manual effort, improves communication accuracy, and provides end-to-end governance, auditability, and distribution management.

## Project Highlights

- Automated population of 12 out of 14 sections (86%) within Major Incident stakeholder communications by retrieving incident data directly from ServiceNow records, significantly reducing manual effort.
- Reduced communication preparation time by approximately 86% (15–20 minutes to 2–3 minutes).
- Centralised communication creation, stakeholder management, distribution, and governance within a single ServiceNow platform.
- Improved communication accuracy, consistency, and stakeholder experience during major incidents.
- Integrated ServiceNow and Outlook to streamline communication workflows and improve operational efficiency.
- Established complete communication auditability to support governance, compliance, and post-incident reviews.

## Business Value

The platform streamlined Major Incident stakeholder communications by automating communication preparation, standardising messaging, and centralising stakeholder distribution management within ServiceNow. This reduced communication preparation time from 15–20 minutes to 2–3 minutes, improved communication accuracy and consistency, and enabled Major Incident Managers to focus on incident coordination, stakeholder engagement, and service restoration activities.

## Problem Statement

Major Incident Managers were required to manually create, populate, maintain, and distribute stakeholder communications during major incidents. The process was time-consuming, prone to human error, and heavily dependent on manual effort, resulting in communication delays and inconsistent stakeholder experiences.

### Key challenges included

- Manual population of incident communication templates resulted in significant effort, increased communication preparation times, and delayed stakeholder updates during major incidents.
- Inconsistent communication quality and formatting across incident managers created varying stakeholder experiences and increased the risk of communication errors.
- Distribution list management was decentralised, reducing visibility, governance, and efficiency in stakeholder communication management.
- Limited governance, auditability, and historical visibility of communications hindered compliance, traceability, and post-incident reviews.
- High operational overhead during critical incidents diverted Major Incident Managers from incident coordination, stakeholder engagement, and service restoration activities.
